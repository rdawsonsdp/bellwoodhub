# Your AI Chief of Staff — An Overview for Mayor Harvey

*Village of Bellwood · Prepared by Robert Dawson, Strategic Data Products · July 2026*

---

## What this is

The Bellwood Hub is your own private chief of staff, built from software. It keeps a
complete, organized copy of your mail and calendar, and it puts a small staff of AI
agents to work on it — reading, sorting, tracking, and drafting — so that when you
open it each morning, the first thing you see is the short list of what actually
needs you.

One principle governs everything in it: **the agents work, you decide.** Nothing is
ever sent, deleted, or promised on your behalf by a machine.

## What it is not

- It **never sends email on its own**. In this first phase, it sends nothing at all —
  even replies you approve are held until you and Robert together decide to turn
  sending on.
- It **cannot delete or change** anything in your mailbox. Its connection is
  read-only; your Gmail and Outlook remain exactly as they are.
- It is **not public**. Only you can log in. It is not an app store product, not
  social media, and your information is not used to train anyone's AI.

## The three screens

**The Wall** — your front page. The top shows the two or three things that need you
right now. Below that sits your cabinet: one card per agent, each showing what it
found, with a green light when that agent is on the job. Tap any card for the full
report — every claim links to the actual email it came from.

**The Queue** — decisions, one at a time. When an agent drafts a reply, it waits
here. You read the full text, then Approve, Fix it (type or speak a correction),
or Skip. Nothing leaves the Queue without your tap.

**Ask** — questions over your whole record, in plain English. "What do we know about
the flooding on Frederick Avenue?" Every answer carries numbered citations pointing
to the exact emails or documents it came from. If the record is silent, it says
"I have no record of that" rather than guessing.

## Your staff, in plain English

Every agent's page in the app answers the same four questions: what it reads, what
it produces, what it can never do, and who decides. The short version of the staff
you'll meet:

| Agent | What it does | What it can never do |
|---|---|---|
| **Outlook & Gmail agents** | Mirror your mailboxes into the record, keep them in sync | Send, delete, or change any email |
| **Schedule** | Shows what's coming up, from your real calendars | Book, move, or cancel anything |
| **Morning Brief** | A short digest: what's waiting, what's open, what's sensitive | Reply to anyone; hide an empty section |
| **Drafting** | Writes replies in your voice, with its reasoning attached | Send without your approval; invent facts |
| **History** | The full story of any person, business, or address | Merge two people without evidence |
| **Sentinel** | Watches every access to the system and flags anything unusual | Block anyone — it flags, a person judges |

## Your data and the public record

- Your information lives in a **database dedicated to you alone** — not shared with
  any other client, demo, or system.
- **Only your accounts can log in.** Robert's team cannot sign in to your Hub, and
  every access to the underlying systems is permanently logged and watched by the
  Sentinel agent.
- The system understands the line between **public record and private life**:
  government mail is treated as public record (FOIA-scoped); anything designated
  private is walled off — never in the public index, never in default search.
- Everything the system does — every retrieval, draft, and decision — lands in an
  **append-only audit ledger** that even the operators cannot edit.

## What happens Tuesday

Setup takes about fifteen minutes, and you do it yourself — that's the point:

1. Open your Hub's address and **sign in with your Google account**. You'll approve
   a standard permission screen; your Gmail begins mirroring into the record.
2. **Sign in with your Microsoft account** the same way; your Outlook connects.
3. That's it. Over the next day the system walks your mailbox history, and your
   agents begin reporting in on their own schedule.

## What we ask of you in the first week

- **Glance at the Wall each morning** — coffee-length, not homework.
- **Work the Queue when drafts appear** — approve, fix, or skip a few. The fixes
  teach the system your voice.
- **Ask it questions** — especially ones you'd normally ask a staffer to dig for.
- **Say what feels wrong.** The feedback button (typed or spoken) goes straight to
  the team. The system improves fastest from your corrections.
