Executive summary

Over the July 19–21 sprint the Hub went from a status page to an intelligence product, and the Mayor's live dashboard was brought to full parity with development. The app now opens on a Chief-of-Staff briefing — "Needs to Know" — that reads the whole record (ranked email issues, actions awaiting approval, upcoming events) and speaks it in a configurable voice. Agent reports read like news briefs with headlines and story updates. Every email reference in the app opens the real message. Mobile is demo-ready after a full readiness audit. All of it is deployed to the Mayor's live site.
